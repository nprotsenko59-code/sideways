# Milestone 7 wanted escalation and living traffic contract

## Wanted heat

- A visible reckless-driving report starts pursuit at heat level one.
- Continued reckless driving while any active police unit has visual contact builds exposure toward heat levels two and three.
- Heat two dispatches a second unit. Heat three dispatches a third unit and deploys a visible, escapable roadblock.
- Units share verified sightings but use staggered intercept targets and separate search waypoints.
- Escaping awards 500 banked points per attained heat level. A bust or successful escape clears heat, removes reinforcements, and retracts the roadblock.

## Living traffic

- Civilian traffic is pre-instantiated and repositioned rather than created during ordinary play.
- A junction claim allows only one traffic car through a road-graph node at a time; approaching cars yield until the claim is released.
- A player impact at or above the configured destruction threshold temporarily wrecks the traffic car. It returns to the pool after the configured delay.
- Near-miss scoring remains collision-free and cannot also fire for a destroyed vehicle encounter.

## Deferred

Dynamic roadblock placement, police radio VO, traffic lights, lane changing, directional intersection priority, persistent vehicle damage, arrest cinematics, and additional police archetypes remain future work.
